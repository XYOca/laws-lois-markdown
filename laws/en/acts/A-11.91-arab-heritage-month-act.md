# Arab Heritage Month Act

> An Act respecting Arab Heritage Month


## Short Title

### 1 Short title

This Act may be cited as the *Arab Heritage Month Act*.

## Arab Heritage Month

### 2 Arab Heritage Month

Throughout Canada, in each year, the month of April is to be known as “Arab Heritage Month”.
